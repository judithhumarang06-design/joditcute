Top Destinations

Tingloy

Masasa Beach

Famous for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, fine-to-coarse sandy/pebbly shoreline, and vibrant marine life ideal for budget-friendly snorkeling.

San Juan

Laiya Beach

A long stretch of wide, developed coastline lined with numerous resorts ranging from budget-friendly options to high-end escapes.

Nasugbu

Fortune Island

Known for its dramatic Grecian-inspired stone pillars perched on coastal cliffs overlooking deep blue waters, offering a dramatic island-escape feel.

Tingloy

Sombrero Island

Named for its distinct hat-like shape, this is a premier spot for island-hopping, swimming, and rich coral reef snorkeling.

Lian

Matabungkay Beach

A classic family-friendly resort destination famous for its floating bamboo rafts (balsas) where guests can lounge, swim, and dine right on the water.

Nasugbu

Calayo Beach

A vibrant public beach with fine light-brown sand, calm shallow waters, and plenty of local water sports like banana boats and jet skis.
