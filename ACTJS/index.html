About this project

This is the portfolio you are looking at right now. I built it to introduce myself, show what I have made in class, and share the little moments in my life.

I started with a simple layout, then added a color palette, sticky navigation, and a bento-style projects grid.

What it does

  • Sticky navigation with smooth scrolling
  • Bento grid for school outputs
  • Responsive layout for phone, tablet, and desktop

What I learned

Planning the layout and colors first made the coding much easier, and I got better at making one design work on every screen size.
